I teach research-oriented, English-language courses in evolutionary computation, machine learning, and autonomous systems. My guiding principle is to “de-blackbox” students: without a deep understanding of how software is implemented, technology risks becoming a black box. I aim to give students the methodological depth, cross-domain knowledge, and critical reflection needed to find new niches where technology creates broad societal value.
