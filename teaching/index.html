Teaching

Research-oriented, English-language courses. My guiding principle is to "de-blackbox" students: without a deep understanding of how software is implemented, technology risks becoming a black box. I aim to give students the methodological depth, cross-domain knowledge, and critical reflection needed to find new niches where technology creates broad societal value.

I teach research-oriented, English-language courses in evolutionary computation, machine learning, and autonomous systems. My approach emphasizes methodological depth, cross-domain reflection, and the ability to critically assess how technology is designed and deployed. Students are encouraged to move beyond surface-level understanding and engage with the underlying mechanisms of the systems they build.
