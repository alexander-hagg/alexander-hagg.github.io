Editorial note

Writing has always been part of the work — to test an argument, to make a position legible, or to think through a problem that a paper cannot hold. These essays are occasional: they appear when something needs saying. Alexander writes them, often in collaboration with AI tools for drafting, editing and revision; he directs the work and signs off on the published text.
