Site Readout

What is a MAP-Elites archive? Instead of searching for one "best" city, we keep the best design for every combination of two goals: how many homes it creates (left→right) and how well it lets cool night air flow through (bottom→top). Each cell is a niche; the colour shows overall quality. This is Quality Diversity: many good, different answers instead of a single winner.

What are the archetypes? The archive is grouped into four recurring design families — a green cold-air finger, a porous courtyard carpet, a stepped windbreak, and a maximum-density block. Click one to see its most representative design.
