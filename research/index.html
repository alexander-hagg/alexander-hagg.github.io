My publications follow a single question through several fields: how can computational systems help people explore large, uncertain design spaces and find diverse, high-quality solutions they can actually use? That work spans quality-diversity search, co-creative AI, surrogate modelling, urban climate, computational chemistry, robotics and elite sports. The full record is also available on Google Scholar and ResearchGate.
