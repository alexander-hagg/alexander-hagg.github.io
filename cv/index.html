Professional & Research Experience

  1. 2026– (18 months)

    Project Leader (Principal Investigator), OpenSKIZZE-Praxis (DBU)

    H-BRS (TREE), Sankt Augustin

    Follow-up to OpenSKIZZE that operationalizes the AI assistant within formal municipal Bauleitplanung (land-use planning) processes. Develops a standardized "climate-resilience building block" — an adapted OpenSKIZZE tool plus action guides and text modules — piloted and validated with the City of Bonn, and published as open-source modules for established GIS (GRASS GIS, QGIS) in cooperation with the Open Source Geospatial Foundation (OSGeo). Partners: Open Source Geospatial Foundation (OSGeo), City of Bonn. Case study

  2. 2025–present

    Co-Lead / Deputy Head, Digital Twin 4 Multiphysics Laboratory (TREE)

    TREE institute, H-BRS, Sankt Augustin

    Interdepartmental research and teaching cooperation between Prof. Dr. Sebastian Houben (Dept. 02) and Prof. Dr. Dirk Reith (Dept. 03) for the collaborative study of multiphysical digital twins at the interface of simulation and physical robotic systems. Case study

  3. 2025–present

    Research Associate, CytoTransport Project (DFG)

    H-BRS, Sankt Augustin

    Interdisciplinary DFG research cluster across Departments 02, 03, and 05 and the IFGA and TREE institutes (with Dr. Karl N. Kirschner). Focus on Human-AI Interaction for molecular conformation prediction, force-field parameter optimization, and predicting the impact of protein mutations. Case study

  4. 2024–2025

    Project Leader (Principal Investigator), OpenSKIZZE (DBU)

    H-BRS, Sankt Augustin

    Interactive, applied AI and co-creative decision support for climate-adapted urban planning at neighborhood level. Responsible for interactive surrogate-assisted multi-solution optimization, integration of the DWD cold-air outflow model KLAM_21, supervision of three students, and an interactive prototype application with workflow and visualization. Partners: Montag Stiftung Urbane Räume gAg, Neue Stadtgärtnerei e.V. Case study

  5. 2023–2025

    Research Associate, UMMBAS Project

    H-BRS, Sankt Augustin

    Interdisciplinary work across Departments 02, 03, and 05 and the IFGA and TREE institutes (with Prof. Dr. Dirk Reith). Human-AI Interaction in scientific applications and collaborative data analysis in a shared immersive VR environment. Co-supervised Lennard-Jones force-field parameterization and generative graph neural networks for molecular conformation generation; developed the virtual screening framework MolFuSE. Case study

  6. 2019–2020

    Project Leader (Principal Investigator), FullDA-FM

    H-BRS, Sankt Augustin · Funded by the VP2 start-up grant

    Simulation- and optimization-assisted decision support in urban planning: built an early prototype including all core machine learning and optimization algorithms and secured follow-up funding. Partners: Montag Stiftung Urbane Räume gAg, Neue Stadtgärtnerei e.V.

  7. 2019–2020

    Research Associate, KISs-BiS Project

    H-BRS (Dept. 02), Sankt Augustin

    With Prof. Dr. Alexander Asteroth. Authored a systematic review of AI/ML in sport and developed proposals to bridge gaps in resource access, expert availability, and method/data handling. Partners: Technical University of Munich, University of Mainz. Case study

  8. 2016–2019

    Research Associate, AErOmAt Project (BMBF)

    H-BRS (Dept. 02), Sankt Augustin

    With Prof. Dr. Dirk Reith and Prof. Dr. Alexander Asteroth. Developed methods for computational creativity, efficient optimization, and problem decomposition to reduce aerodynamic simulations, applied to airfoils, recumbent bicycles, a Formula Student racing car, cabin airflow, and passenger car side mirrors. Partners: DLR, Fraunhofer SCAI, University of Siegen, SIDACT GmbH, Beyss Leichtfahrzeuge (Go-One). Case study

  9. 2014–2015

    Research Associate, E-LaBoR Project

    H-BRS (Dept. 02), Sankt Augustin

    With Prof. Dr. Stefanie Meilinger, Prof. Dr. Alexander Asteroth, and IZNE. Developed a phased rollout strategy for charging infrastructure in Bonn and the Rhein-Sieg district, plus a planning guide for detailed siting. Partners: City of Bonn, Rhein-Sieg District, SWB Energie und Wasser, rhenag, Stadtwerke Troisdorf, RheinEnergie, RWE. Case study

NIFTi researched cooperative teamwork between humans and robots in urban search-and-rescue scenarios to reduce operator cognitive load and optimize joint team workflow.
